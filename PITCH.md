# FixGuide AI — pitch outline (Demo Day, Dec 3)

**Track:** 04 · AI Assistants & Work (Claude integration)
**One-liner:** The repair guidebook and AI assistant a phone technician can use
with both hands inside the phone.

## Problem (30s)

- Phone repair is a two-hands, eyes-on-the-board job. Every time a technician
  needs the next step, a torque spec, or a diagnosis check, they put down the
  tweezers, pick up a phone or tablet, unlock it, scroll — with flux on their
  fingers.
- Repair knowledge lives in PDFs, YouTube videos and forum threads: unusable
  mid-repair, at the bench, hands busy.
- Result: slower repairs, skipped safety checks (swollen batteries, wet
  boards), and knowledge that never reaches the moment of work.

## Solution (60s)

- **FixGuide AI on G2:** pick a repair, get one step at a time in your field
  of view — diagnosis first, exact tool, safety warning, progress bar.
- **Hands-free control:** tap = next step, double-tap = back, ring scroll =
  browse, hold = talk.
- **Ask Claude mid-repair:** "current draw is 0.05 amps, what now?" → a short,
  glanceable answer with the next measurement to take. Keys stay on the
  backend; the glasses only ever see text.
- **Write by voice:** "note replaced the charge port" → saved to the job log
  with steps completed and duration. The repair documents itself.
- **Offline-safe:** without network/keys, the built-in guide knowledge base
  still answers — labeled honestly as offline mode.

## Why glasses (30s)

- The bench is the one workplace where a screen you don't hold wins: hands
  occupied, eyes on small parts, gloves/flux make touchscreens hostile.
- G2's monochrome glanceable display + mic + ring is exactly the input/output
  budget of a repair step: a few words, one action, zero phone pickups.
- This is "read and write" done right: read the step, act on it, log it —
  without leaving the work.

## Demo (live, 60s)

1. Menu → Not Charging → 3 steps by tap (tool + warning callouts).
2. Voice note → saved.
3. Voice question → Claude answer → scroll.
4. Finish → JOB DONE → Job Log entry.

## Market & moat (20s)

- Millions of independent repair techs and shop benches worldwide; Emilio
  Tecnologías already publishes repair guides in 4 languages — the content
  pipeline exists today.
- Roadmap: per-model guides, schematic-reading mode (diode-mode values),
  shop job tickets via Telegram bot, Spanish/Portuguese/Arabic editions.
